## IResourceLoadingOptions.WhitelistedResources property

Absolute URI prefixes of external resources that are loaded while [`SkipExternalResources`](../skipexternalresources) is true, for example `https://cdn.example.com/assets/` or `file:///C:/assets/`.

### Remarks

A resource matches an entry when scheme, host (exact, no subdomains) and port are equal and its path starts with the entry's path at a `/` boundary; paths are compared case-sensitively after dot segments are removed. Local and UNC paths are compared as `file:` URIs. Relative and protocol-relative (`//host/...`) references never match an `http` or `https` entry. An entry that is empty, not an absolute URI, carries a query or fragment, or contains `%2F`, `%5C` or `%2E` is rejected with ArgumentException when the [`Merger`](../../../groupdocs.merger/merger) is created. When an allowed resource redirects to another location, the redirect is followed.
